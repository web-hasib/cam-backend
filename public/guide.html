How to Use PhoneCam as a Wireless HD Studio Webcam

Transform your smartphone into an ultra-low latency 1080p studio webcam for your computer in seconds.

3-Step Quick Start

1

Launch PhoneCam Studio on Desktop

Open the PhoneCam Desktop software on your PC or Mac. You will see a unique QR Code and Session Code (e.g. WGXW-BLJ3) on the screen.

2

Scan QR Code with Your Phone

You can connect using any of these simple methods:

  • Method A (Easiest): Open your standard phone Camera app and point it at the desktop screen. Tap the link to open directly in your browser.
  • Method B (Built-in Scanner): Open phonecam website and use the built-in scanner to scan the code.
  • Method C (Manual Entry): Type the 8-character session code shown on your desktop screen.
💡 No account or sign-up needed!
3

Stream to OBS, Zoom, Discord & Meet

Your camera will connect instantly via high-speed WebRTC P2P. On your computer, select PhoneCam Virtual Camera in your favorite video conference or streaming software.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do my phone and computer need to be on the same Wi-Fi?
For the best ultra-low latency, being on the same local Wi-Fi network is recommended. However, PhoneCam also supports encrypted STUN/TURN relay servers for remote connections.
How do I install PhoneCam as an App on my phone?
On iOS Safari, tap the Share button (⎋) and select Add to Home Screen. On Android Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu and select Install App.
What if I want to disconnect or switch computers?
Tap the red Exit / Disconnect button anytime. Confirm the prompt, and then choose "Scan Another QR" to immediately connect to another computer screen.
